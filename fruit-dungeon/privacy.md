# Fruit Dungeon: privacy policy

*Last updated: 8 October 2026*

Fruit Dungeon is a game for Android phones, made by Mārcis Čaupjonoks
("I" below). This page says what the game keeps, what its ads collect,
and the choices you have.

## What the game keeps: on your phone only

The game remembers your run in progress, your settings, your records
(wins, best floors, the last runs, the monsters you've met) and your
achievements. All of it stays **on your phone**. The game has no
accounts, asks for no name or email, and has no server of its own: I
never receive any of it.

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings,
deletes all of it. If Android's backup is on, your phone may include the
game's data in its backup to your Google account, like any other app's.
That backup is between you and Google.

## Ads

The game shows an ad only when you ask for one: after a defeat, you may
choose to watch an ad to revive. Nothing else in the game needs it, and
the game plays the same without it.

The ads come from **Google AdMob**. To choose ads, measure them and
prevent fraud, Google's ad software inside the game collects the
following and shares it with Google, encrypted in transit:

- your **IP address**, from which an approximate location can be guessed;
- **device identifiers**: your phone's advertising ID and app set ID;
- **how you interact** with the ads and the game (for example, that an
  ad was shown and watched);
- **diagnostic information**: crashes and how the ads perform.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How Google uses data from apps
that show its ads: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## Your choices

- **In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland,** the game
  asks for your consent the first time, before any ad is loaded. To
  change your answer later, open **Settings → Privacy options** in the
  game.
- **Everywhere,** you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or ask
  for ads that aren't personalized, in your phone's settings (on most
  phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- **Not watching ads** is always fine: the revive is the only thing an
  ad gives.

## Children

The game is not meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly
collect anything from them. The game itself collects nothing (see
above).
